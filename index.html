Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Mumbai University CGPA to percentage?

The commonly published Mumbai University engineering formula is Percentage = 7.1 × CGPA + 11. Rules can differ by faculty and scheme, so always confirm with your official marksheet or the university circular.

What is the SPPU CGPA to percentage formula?

SPPU circular no. 332 of 2020 gives grade-band equations (for example 10 × CGPA − 7.5 for the A band, CGPA 6.75 to 8.24). A simpler (CGPA − 0.75) × 10 rule is also widely quoted. UniCalc India lets you choose and shows the equation used.

What is the VTU CGPA to percentage formula?

The most widely quoted VTU formula is Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10. Some sources list CGPA × 10 for newer schemes, so verify against your VTU marksheet.

How is AKTU CGPA converted to percentage?

Published sources differ: some give Percentage = (CGPA × 10) − 7.5, others CGPA × 10. UniCalc India supports both and shows the formula used. Check your AKTU result document.

How is age calculated for government exams like SSC and UPSC?

Age is counted from your date of birth to the cut-off date given in the official notification, expressed in completed years, months and days. Use the cut-off date from the notice, not today's date.

Are these calculator results official?

No. Results are for guidance only. Universities and recruiters accept the percentage stated on your official marksheet or conversion certificate.
