How to Get a CGPA to Percentage Certificate from Your University

Updated October 2026 • About 6 minute read

Note: Procedures, fees and timelines differ from one university and college to another. Use this guide as a checklist and confirm the details with your own examination section.

Your marksheet says 8.2 CGPA, but the application form wants a percentage. Sooner or later most Indian students who study under a grading system hit this wall. Online calculators can give you an estimate, yet they cannot give you an authoritative number. For that you need an official CGPA to percentage conversion certificate from your institution. This guide explains why it matters, the step-by-step process, the documents you will need, and how long it usually takes.

What is a CGPA to percentage certificate?

It is a document issued by your university or college that states the percentage equivalent of your CGPA (or SGPA) and, ideally, the formula used to derive it. It carries an official signature or stamp, which is why recruiters and admission offices trust it more than a number you calculated yourself.

Why you need one

1. Jobs and campus placements

Many companies set eligibility cut-offs such as "60% throughout" or "minimum 65% in graduation". If your marksheet shows only CGPA, the HR team must convert it, and different recruiters may use different formulas. A certificate removes the guesswork and protects you from being rejected because of a conversion dispute.

2. Higher studies, including MS abroad

Foreign universities and credential evaluation services often ask for percentage or an equivalent on your academic record. Some will evaluate your grades using their own scale, while others ask for a conversion statement from your institution. Having a certificate ready, along with your transcripts, avoids delays in an application where deadlines are strict.

3. Government exams and recruitment

Some recruitment notices specify a minimum percentage in graduation or post-graduation. When your result is in CGPA, the notice may require a certificate from the university stating the equivalent percentage, and it may ask you to produce it at document verification. If you cannot, your candidature can be at risk even after clearing the written exam.

4. Scholarships, fellowships and other forms

Scholarship portals and fellowship programmes frequently use a percentage cut-off. An official certificate settles the figure once and for all.

Step-by-step process to apply

  1. Find the issuing office. In most affiliated colleges, the examination section of your college handles this. In some universities, the university's examination or results department issues it. Check the notice board, the college website or ask your department office.
  2. Check the formula your university uses. Look at your marksheet for a conversion note and at university circulars. Knowing the formula in advance helps you verify the certificate when you receive it.
  3. Write an application. Address it to the head of the examination section or the Controller of Examinations. Mention your full name, programme, branch, seat number or PRN, year of passing, and why you need the certificate.
  4. Attach your documents. Submit photocopies of marksheets and keep the originals ready for verification (see the list below).
  5. Pay the fee, if applicable. Some institutions charge a nominal fee and some do not. Ask for a receipt and keep it safe.
  6. Track your application. Note the date of submission and any reference number. Follow up politely if the expected timeline passes.
  7. Check the certificate before leaving the counter. Verify your name spelling, seat number, CGPA, the calculated percentage, the formula, and the signature and seal.

Documents usually required

  • Written application stating the purpose of the certificate
  • Photocopies of marksheets or grade cards for all semesters, or the consolidated marksheet
  • Degree certificate or provisional certificate, if already issued
  • Photo ID such as Aadhaar, passport or driving licence
  • College ID card (for current students and recent graduates)
  • Fee receipt, where a fee is charged
  • Passport-size photographs, if the office asks for them

Requirements vary, so ask your examination section for the exact checklist before you travel to the campus.

How long does it take?

There is no single national timeline. One University of Mumbai-affiliated engineering college states on its website that a CGPA or SGPA to percentage certificate takes about 4 to 5 working days from the date of application. Other institutions may take one to two weeks, and requests that need the university's approval can take longer, especially near admission seasons when thousands of students apply at once.

A sensible rule is to apply at least three to four weeks before your deadline. If you need the certificate urgently, ask the office whether an expedited option exists, and never wait until the last week.

Tips to avoid problems

  • Apply early. Exam sections get busy around result and admission periods.
  • Request extra copies. If you are applying to several universities or employers, ask for more than one original, or for attested copies.
  • Match your name everywhere. Your name on the certificate, marksheet and ID should be identical. Fix discrepancies before applying abroad.
  • Ask for the formula in writing. A certificate that states how the percentage was derived is more convincing than one that gives only a number.
  • Scan and store a copy. Keep a clear PDF for online applications, but retain the original for verification rounds.
  • Do not self-certify. Typing your own calculated percentage into a form can lead to rejection if it does not match the official figure.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need a CGPA to percentage certificate?

Many employers, foreign universities and recruitment notices ask for a percentage, while your marksheet shows only grades or CGPA. An official certificate states the equivalent percentage and the formula used, so your figure is accepted as authentic.

How long does it take to get a CGPA to percentage certificate?

It varies by institution. One Mumbai-affiliated college states about 4 to 5 working days, while others may take one to two weeks or longer during busy periods. Apply early and ask the exam section for the expected timeline.

What documents are required for a CGPA to percentage certificate?

Typically you need a written application stating the purpose, copies of your marksheets or grade cards, a photo ID and your college ID. Some institutions also ask for a fee receipt. Check with your exam section for the exact list.
