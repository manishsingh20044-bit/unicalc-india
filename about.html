About UniCalc India

Welcome to UniCalc India, your reliable destination for accurate academic and exam-related calculators. Our goal is simple: to help Indian college students and government exam aspirants get quick, accurate results without any confusion.

We understand that converting CGPA to percentage can be confusing, especially when different universities have different formulas. That's why our tools show you exactly which formula is being used and provide clear disclaimers.

Whether you are checking your eligibility for a job, preparing for higher studies, or verifying your age for an SSC exam, we are here to make the process simple and stress-free.

All our tools are 100% free to use, run directly in your browser, and require no sign-up.
